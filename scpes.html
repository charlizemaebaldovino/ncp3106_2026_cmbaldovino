01 WHAT IS SCPES? +

SCPES stands for the Society of Computer Engineering Students, the student organization serving the Computer Engineering community at UE Manila.

02 WHAT DOES SCPES DO? +

SCPES organizes technical seminars, student activities, community initiatives, professional development opportunities, and other programs for Computer Engineering students.

03 HOW CAN STUDENTS PARTICIPATE? +

Students can participate in SCPES programs, events, seminars, activities, projects, and other opportunities announced by the organization.

04 WHO LEADS SCPES? +

SCPES is led by its student executive officers, with different positions responsible for internal affairs, events, technical operations, media, outreach, logistics, and other areas.
