ABOUT SCPES

MORE THAN AN
ORGANIZATION.

The Society of Computer Engineering Students brings together Computer Engineering students through academic, technical, social, and community-centered initiatives.

SCPES provides opportunities for students to connect with one another, develop their abilities, participate in meaningful activities, and contribute to the Computer Engineering community.
