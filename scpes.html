01 THE ORGANIZATION

The Society of Computer Engineering Students brings together the Computer Engineering community of UE Manila through collaboration, learning, leadership, and shared experiences. It provides students with opportunities to connect with one another, develop their skills, participate in meaningful activities, and grow beyond the classroom.

SCPES is not only about organizing events, attending workshops, or completing activities. It is also about the people we meet, the friendships we build, and the moments we share along the way.
