DEFINITION

WHAT IS
COMPUTER ENGINEERING?

“

Computer Engineering (Comp Eng or CpE) is a branch of engineering that combines computer science and electrical engineering.

It focuses on the design, development, implementation, maintenance, and integration of both hardware and software in modern computer systems and computer-controlled devices.
