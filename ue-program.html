01
THE PROGRAM

Computer Engineering at UE Manila.

The Computer Engineering program combines principles of electrical engineering and computer science to prepare students for the design, development, and application of modern computing technologies.

Students develop knowledge in programming, electronics, computer architecture, embedded systems, networking, and other areas of computer engineering.

Through laboratory activities, technical projects, and collaborative work, students are given opportunities to apply engineering concepts to practical problems.
