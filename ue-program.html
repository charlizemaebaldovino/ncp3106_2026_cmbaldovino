OVERVIEW OF THE PROGRAM

ENGINEERING THE
DIGITAL WORLD.

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering program at the University of the East focuses on the integration of computer hardware and software.

Students explore areas such as embedded systems, computer networking, automation, and intelligent systems while developing the knowledge and technical skills needed for careers in the computing and electronics industries.
